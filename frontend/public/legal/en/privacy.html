Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Tangent is a personal wealth aggregator. This policy describes which personal data we process, for what purposes, on what legal basis, and with whom it is shared. It applies to the service available at https://riskybusinesses.uk and to the "Tangent" iPhone app, which uses the same account and the same server.

In short: Tangent collects the minimum necessary (an email address to identify your account, and bank data only through PSD2 (« DSP2 » in French) if you choose to connect it). No data is sold, shared for advertising purposes, or used to train AI models.

2. Data collected

2.1 Account data

  • Email address (required) — account identifier, transactional communication (password reset).
  • Password — stored hashed (Argon2id), never in clear text, never readable even by the administrator.
  • Display name (optional) — text you enter to personalise the interface.
  • Linked OAuth accounts — if you sign in with Google or with Apple, we store the stable identifier the provider gives us (sub) and the email address it passes on to us (Apple may offer you a relay address), together with an encrypted token (Fernet) that we use for no purpose other than verifying your identity.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (iPhone app) — biometric unlocking is handled by iOS on your phone. No biometric data is transmitted to Tangent or stored by it.

2.2 Bank data (if you connect it)

  • Account metadata (names, partial IBAN, account type, balance, currency) obtained through a PSD2-authorised aggregator: Powens, or Enable Banking for banks Powens does not cover.
  • Bank transaction lines (date, label, amount, category).
  • Investment details (ISIN, quantity, valuation) where present.
  • Details of outstanding loans (remaining principal, rate, monthly payment, due dates).
  • Powens authentication tokens and Enable Banking session identifiers encrypted at rest (Fernet) — used only to refresh data from your bank.

2.3 Manually entered data

The content of your portfolio, your financial profile and any configuration you freely enter in the interface. You are its sole author and sole owner.

2.4 History of your data (archive)

Twice a day, Tangent records a snapshot of what it knows about your wealth (accounts, positions, spending, results of the method) so that it can later show you how it has evolved. These records are encrypted at rest (Fernet) with a key that is not stored in the database; they are included in the export of your data and deleted with your account.

2.5 Technical cookies

Three strictly necessary cookies are used. No advertising or analytics cookies.

Name Purpose Duration

2.6 Server logs

For diagnostic purposes, we keep technical logs (IP address, timestamp, HTTP route, status code) for 30 rolling days.

6. Security

  • Passwords hashed with Argon2id (OWASP recommendation).
  • OAuth and bank session tokens (Powens, Enable Banking, Google, Apple) and the archive encrypted at rest with Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256) using separate keys.
  • iPhone app: locked with Face ID / Touch ID, screen hidden in the app switcher, third-party sign-ins in the system's secure browser.
  • Communications encrypted with TLS 1.3 (terminated by Cloudflare).
  • Security headers enabled: HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options.
  • Session cookies HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax, Secure in production.
  • Strict per-user isolation (row-level multi-tenancy) — one user technically cannot access another user's data.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (« RGPD » in French; articles 15 to 22) and the Loi Informatique et Libertés (the French Data Protection Act), you have the following rights:

  • Access to your personal data;
  • Rectification of inaccurate data;
  • Erasure of your account and all associated data;
  • Portability of your data in a structured format (JSON);
  • Objection to processing on legitimate grounds;
  • Restriction of processing;
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time (without retroactive effect).

To exercise these rights, write to privacy@riskybusinesses.uk from your account's email address. We reply within 30 days at most.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés, the French data protection authority).

8. Transfers outside the European Union

Some processors (Google, Apple, Resend) are based in the United States. Data transfers to these recipients are governed by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), which guarantee an equivalent level of protection.
